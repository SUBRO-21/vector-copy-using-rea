HOW TO PLAY

You're running for your life across the rooftops. Stay ahead of the hunter and reach the finish.

ActionTouchKeyboard

  • Crates & desks: jump just in front of them to vault without losing speed. Speed Vault, Dash Vault, Thief Vault and Reverse Vault over crates, Kong or Monkey Vault over desks, Double Kong over long tables. You can also slide under desks. Run into one and you stumble.
  • Hazard beams: slide under them.
  • Swing bars over wide gaps: jump to grab, do a giant swing, and fly across with a backflip.
  • Yellow arrows: swipe right on them to sprint, which pulls you away from the hunter.
  • Big drops: land from high up and you roll automatically.
  • Walls: jump into a wall to climb it. Walls that are too tall stop you dead.
  • Glowing trick zones: jump from inside one to do a trick. Tricks score points and push the hunter back.
  • Data cubes: collect them for stars. 3 stars = finish + 70% of cubes + every trick.
